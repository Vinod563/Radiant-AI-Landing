Recommended Next Step
Where to Focus First
The lowest-scoring dimension points to the highest-leverage action.
Priority action — People & Governance
Publish a GenAI acceptable-use policy before your next deployment. It should cover: approved tools, prohibited use cases, data handling rules, and a process for requesting new tools. Two pages. Board-approved. Distributed to every employee. This single document unblocks enterprise adoption faster than any technology investment.
